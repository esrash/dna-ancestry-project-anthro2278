INTRODUCTION

DNA ancestry tests
and racial identity

If your ancestry estimate changed, would your identity change too?

This research blog explores what ancestry tests can tell us about our genetic background, the limits of those estimates, and how people interpret their results alongside family history, race, ethnicity, and cultural belonging.

The central argument

DNA results can inform family research. Their meaning emerges through interpretation, family knowledge, and social experience. (Royal et al., 2010; Roth & Ivemark, 2018)
